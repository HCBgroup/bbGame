# Bounce Back — MVP storyboard

Panels for the story in [STORY.md](STORY.md). Art and music notes are cues for those lanes. They are not files to generate from an agent.

Read every panel in order. Panel ids are stable. Do not renumber them. Do not insert a panel without a new id at the end of a beat.

Player verbs allowed in the whole game: walk, press Space, mash Space. Nothing else.

## Beat A — House

### A1. Alarm

- **See:** Bedroom, morning, lights low. Arun on the bed. Alarm buzzing.
- **Do:** Space gets him up.
- **Hear:** A thin alarm, then room tone.
- **Line:** none.

### A2. Mirror

- **See:** Bathroom or a mirror in the bedroom. His reflection.
- **Do:** Walk up. Space.
- **Line (Arun):** "Don't lie again today."
- **Art:** One reflection is enough. He does not need a new outfit.

### A3. Shoes and bag

- **See:** Shoes by the door. Bag on a chair, looking empty.
- **Do:** Space on the shoes, Space on the bag. Order does not matter.
- **Line (Arun, bag):** "Still empty. She thinks I revised."

### A4. Mom

- **See:** Kitchen. Mom with keys, about to leave.
- **Do:** Space to talk. Space to advance. Then the front door unlocks.
- **Line (Mom):** "You studied?"
- **Line (Arun):** "I'm going."
- **Line (Mom):** "Eat on the way. And come home."

### A5. Leave

- **See:** The front door, street light outside.
- **Do:** Space on the door. Go to school.
- **Hear:** Door, then the school loop starts later, not here.

## Beat B — The exam

### B1. The question

- **See:** Classroom. Ms. Das. One question on the board. Arun at a desk.
- **Do:** Space to start. No walking required if he already stands at the desk.
- **Line (Ms. Das):** "One question. The one from last night's page."
- **Line (Arun):** "The page I never opened."

### B2. The fail

- **See:** Arun's head down. The board still has the question. Color is normal until he gives up.
- **Do:** Space.
- **Line (Arun):** "It's gone. There's nothing there."
- This fail is the story. Do not make the player lose a minigame to see it.

### B3. Kabir sits down

- **See:** Kabir pulls a chair to the side of the desk. Not center stage.
- **Do:** Space through two lines.
- **Line (Kabir):** "I went blank in this room last year."
- **Line (Kabir):** "Stand up and say one answer. A bad one still counts."

### B4. The bar

- **See:** The sliding bar. The question still on the board. Kabir stays in the shot.
- **Do:** Space when the marker is in the green. The green zone is the wider of the two checks.
- **Hit line (Arun):** "It's the second one. I think."
- **Hit line (Ms. Das):** "Sit. We're moving on."
- **Miss:** Go to B5, then come back to this panel. Do not change the question.

### B5. Fall and bounce

- **See:** Screen shakes, then grey. Arun on the ground. A bounce bar at the bottom.
- **Do:** Mash Space until the bar fills. He stands. Color comes back.
- **Hear:** A dull thud, then a small boing when he is up.
- **Line (Kabir):** "Again."

## Beat C — Chore, then the creative task

### C1. The papers

- **See:** Three loose papers on three desks. Ms. Das at the front.
- **Do:** Walk. Space on each paper. Space at her desk to hand them over.
- **Line (Ms. Das):** "Those, on my desk."
- **Line (Arun, after the second paper):** "Last week they laughed. Keep walking."
- No bar. No fall. If he has all three, she takes them.

### C2. The picture

- **See:** Ms. Das holds up one picture. The class faces Arun.
- **Do:** Space.
- **Line (Ms. Das):** "One sentence. What is this about?"
- **Line (Arun):** "They're all looking."

### C3. Kabir, from the side

- **See:** Kabir near the wall, not next to the teacher.
- **Do:** Space.
- **Line (Kabir):** "Don't perform. Point at one true thing."

### C4. The bar, tighter

- **See:** Same bar. Smaller green zone than B4.
- **Do:** Space in the green.
- **Hit line (Arun):** "Somebody left, and the room stayed."
- **Hit line (Ms. Das):** "That's a sentence. Bell's about to go."
- **Miss:** Same fall as B5, Kabir's "Again.", then this bar returns.
- The picture's meaning is that one sentence. Do not write a second correct answer.

### C5. The bell

- **See:** Corridor or the classroom door.
- **Do:** Space on the door home.
- **Line (Ms. Das):** "Results tonight. Go home."
- She does not say the score.

## Beat D — Rooftop

### D1. Up the stairs

- **See:** Stairs in his house, warmer light than the morning. No Mom in this beat.
- **Do:** Walk onto the roof.

### D2. Thinking

- **See:** Roof, evening. Arun sitting or standing at the edge, feet safe, not a joke about falling. City or neighborhood behind him. Phone in his hand, dark.
- **Do:** Space through three thoughts.
- **Line (Arun):** "I told her I revised."
- **Line (Arun):** "I froze, and then I didn't."
- **Line (Arun):** "If this says fail, I stay the kid who can't get up."

### D3. The phone

- **See:** The phone screen, big enough to read. It says he passed. No percentage, no rank, no subject list.
- **Do:** Space to open it. Space to continue.
- **On the phone:** PASSED
- **Line (Arun):** "Not first. Passed."

### D4. End

- **See:** He sits. The roof stays. Then the end card.
- **Card:** "He passed. Tomorrow he can tell Mom the truth."
- **Do:** Space does nothing except leave the end card if you need a way back to the title. No post-credits scene.

## What each person makes from this

- **Art:** rooms for A, B/C, and D. Arun awake, head-down, on the ground, standing. Mom, Ms. Das, Kabir. One exam board, one picture, one phone screen, three papers. Title card can be his back at the roof.
- **Music:** morning room, school, grey-fall sting, boing, a quieter roof. The phone moment can be almost silent.
- **Code:** walk, Space, the bar twice, the grey mash, door from house to school to roof. Panel ids are the order. Not a request to invent new beats.
