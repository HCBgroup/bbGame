# Bounce Back — MVP story

This is the only story document. The playable storyboard is in [STORYBOARD.md](STORYBOARD.md). If a line here and a panel there disagree, this file wins.

No code, scenes, art files, or audio belong in this document. It is the story the team is making at Haven.

## The promise

A short top-down day in one kid's life. He starts at home, fails at school, gets talked back onto his feet, finishes the day, and on the roof he finds out he passed. It takes a few minutes. Failing a button press never ends the game.

Working title: **Bounce Back**.

The kid's name in the script is **Arun**. Change that name in this file only, then copy it everywhere else. Do not add a second protagonist.

## What the MVP is

Four beats. Same places. No extra days, no inventory, no quest log, no save, no multiple endings.

| Beat | Place | What the player does | Can he fail the button? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Get ready | His house, morning | Walk. Press Space on the mirror, the shoes, the bag, and Mom. Then leave. | No |
| 2. The exam | Classroom | He is already stuck. Kabir talks him back up. A sliding bar is the answer. | Yes. Miss, fall, mash Space, try the bar again. |
| 3. The papers, then the creative task | Same school | Walk the papers to the teacher's desk. Then the creative task, same fall-and-retry as the exam. | Papers: no. Creative task: yes. |
| 4. The result | Rooftop, evening | He thinks. He opens his phone. The result says he passed. | No |

The sliding bar is the only skill check. It is used twice: the exam, then the creative task. Green zone is a bit smaller the second time. A miss turns the screen grey. Mashing Space fills a bar, he stands up, and the same check comes back.

## People

- **Arun**, the kid. Tired, not cruel, not a joke. He lies when he is scared, then hates that he lied.
- **Mom**, at home. She is already dressed for work. She is sharp because she is worried, not because she is the villain.
- **Ms. Das**, the teacher. She wants the work. She does not make a speech.
- **Kabir**, a senior who shows up when Arun folds. He failed a year ago and came back. He is the motivator. He does not do the task for Arun. One sprite is enough. He only enters on the two school checks.

Nobody else gets a scene. Classmates can be shapes in the room. They do not talk.

## The three problems in his life

These are lines and pictures, not new systems. Do not add a menu for them.

1. **The lie.** Last night Mom asked if he revised. He said yes. His notes are still blank. Getting ready hurts because the bag is light.
2. **The freeze.** Last time he had to speak in front of the class, he went silent. Somebody laughed. He is sure it will happen again.
3. **The result.** He thinks one bad phone screen will prove he cannot get back up. The roof is where that thought is loudest.

A fight with a friend, money, or a romance is out. Do not add them today.

## Story

### House, morning

Arun wakes to an alarm he already snoozed once. The room is dim. He is not late yet. He will be, if he keeps sitting there.

He gets up. At the mirror he splashes his face and does not like what he sees: a boy who is about to pretend again. The shoes are by the door. The bag is on the chair, too light, because the revision was a lie.

Mom is in the kitchen, keys in her hand. She asks, not unkindly, "You studied?"

He does not confess. He says "I'm going." She tells him to eat, then lets him go. She is not fooled. She is out of time.

He leaves the house. The school day starts.

### School

The classroom is already half full. Ms. Das puts one question on the board. It is not a full exam. It is the question he blanked on while revising nothing.

Arun reads it and goes empty. That is the fail. It happens in the scene before the player aims the bar. Kabir pulls a chair over, quiet enough that it is not a show. He says he froze like this last year, and the only way back was to answer once, out loud, even badly.

The bar slides. If Arun hits it, he gives the answer. It does not need to be brilliant. Ms. Das nods and moves on. If he misses, he folds, the color drops out, and he has to stand back up before the bar returns. Kabir stays until the answer lands.

Ms. Das then tells him to collect the loose papers from three desks and bring them to her. It is a chore, not a test. He walks. While he does it he hears the laugh from last week in his head, one short line, and keeps walking.

Then the creative task. Ms. Das holds up a picture and asks him to say what it is about, in one sentence, in front of the others. He feels the freeze coming. Kabir, from the side, tells him to aim at one true thing, not a performance. Same bar, a little stricter. Success is one plain sentence. The class does not cheer. The task is done.

The bell goes. Ms. Das says the results come this evening. She does not tell him the score. He is allowed to go home.

### Rooftop, evening

He climbs to the roof of his own house. This is where he sits when the rooms feel small. He thinks, in three short lines, about the lie, the freeze, and the phone he has not opened.

He opens it. The screen says he passed. Not first. Passed.

He sits down instead of shouting. The last line of the game is his, quiet: he can tell Mom the truth tonight. The game ends there. Tomorrow is not a scene.

## Ending card

Show this, then stop.

**He passed.**

Tomorrow he can tell Mom the truth.

## Tone

Short lines. No speech about believing in yourself. Kabir sounds like an older kid, not a poster. Mom sounds tired. Ms. Das sounds busy. Arun sounds scared and then a bit steadier. Nothing mean about failing. Nothing that says his life is fixed.

## Out of the MVP

Do not add these, even as ideas in dialogue:

- A second day, a boss, a score, or a grade letter higher than "passed"
- A friend he must apologize to on the roof
- Choices that change the ending
- A phone menu, chats, or more than one result screen
- Quests besides Get Ready, the exam, the papers, and the creative task
- Any character besides Arun, Mom, Ms. Das, and Kabir
